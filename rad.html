The RAD Network

Queer professionals, matched with care.

RAD is a matchmaking service between institutions and queer professionals. Everyone queer is welcome, and we especially encourage trans and non-binary professionals to join. You tell us what you need, we introduce the right people, with their consent, every time.

For institutions For professionals

How it works

Tell us what you need

A photographer for your symposium, a coach for your PhD students, a designer for your poster: send us a short request with the date, place and budget.

Meet your match

We pick one to three professionals from the network and ask each of them first. Only those who say yes are introduced to you.

Hire directly

You agree on the job and pay the professional directly, at their own rates. They keep 100% of their fee. Once the match works out, we charge you a small fee. If it doesn't, we find someone else at no extra cost.

Our promises to professionals

Always free

No membership fee, no commission. Institutions and sponsors fund the network.

Your consent, every time

We ask you before every single introduction. You can say no without giving a reason.

You decide what's shared

Private introductions only, or also featured in our newsletter for institutions. Change your mind anytime, or leave and have all your data deleted.

Questions

Can institutions see the list of members?

No. The member list stays private. You describe what you need, and we introduce suitable people who have agreed to it.

Will the client know I'm queer, trans or non-binary?

Only if you want them to. You decide, and you can decide differently for each job.

Why does the institution pay, and not the professional?

Institutions save time and get a trusted, respectful way to work with queer professionals. Professionals should keep everything they earn.

Where does RAD operate?

We start in Frankfurt and Rhein-Main, and match remote work across Germany. More regions will follow.
